ABOUT LIVEKEN

POWERING TRADE THROUGH SMART LOGISTICS.

LIVEKEN LOGISTICS is a logistics company concept positioned around the Port of Mombasa, Kenya, one of East Africa's major maritime trade gateways.

We focus on connecting businesses, suppliers and customers through efficient cargo transportation, freight forwarding, warehousing and supply chain solutions.
